## Profile

Computational chemist specializing in **reaction mechanisms**, with a strong international profile, proven research independence, and growing expertise in **data science** and **AI/ML**. I obtained my PhD in 2019 from the University of Vigo (UVIGO, *cum laude*) under an FPU fellowship, with a thesis on mechanistic studies of **molybdenum-promoted oxygen atom transfer** reactions relevant to sustainable chemistry.

From 2019 to 2023, I worked at the University of Groningen (RUG, The Netherlands), where I established and led the computational activity within an experimental catalysis group. My postdoctoral work focused on (i) **copper-catalyzed enantioselective dearomatization** of N-heteroaromatics and (ii) **manganese-catalyzed hydrofunctionalization** of α,β-unsaturated compounds, contributing to cost-effective and sustainable approaches to chiral molecules.

Since 2023, I have developed a line in **astrochemistry** at CiQUS (USC) on automated mechanistic elucidation of (poly)aromatic hydrocarbon formation in the interstellar medium (ISM), including work on barrierless reaction networks leading to benzene and other C6 rings (**Science Advances**, 2024). In parallel, I have accumulated extensive teaching and supervision experience (bachelor/master projects; 500+ teaching hours). In 2025–2026, I contribute at CESGA to **Dem Ms DATAlife** (One Health data space demonstrator; >3M€), integrating data analysis workflows and AI components.

**Selected indicators (as of 2025-12-09, as reported in the source CV):** ~29 peer‑reviewed publications (28 published + 1 submitted) and one conference proceeding; ~500 citations; h‑index 13 (Scopus); ~55% open access outputs; participation in ~20 conferences; positive ANECA evaluation for Full Professor (2025); and RyC evaluation (2022) with 83.60 points.
